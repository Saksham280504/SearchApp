# Compound Categories Glossary

## Industrial & Environmental
- **Environmental Pollutant**: Chemicals that degrade ecosystems, often highly persistent (e.g., PFAS, heavy metals).
- **Industrial / Waste**: Byproducts of manufacturing processes, including effluents.
- **PFAS (Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances)**: Known as "Forever Chemicals" because they do not break down in the environment. Widely used in non-stick coatings, firefighting foams, and waterproofing. Highly toxic to liver and immune systems.
- **Plasticizer**: Chemicals (like Phthalates or BPA) added to plastics to increase flexibility. Often endocrine disruptors.

## Personal & Medical
- **Pharmaceuticals & Drugs**: Active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) often found in wastewater and STP (Sewage Treatment Plant) effluents because they are excreted by humans.
- **Antibiotic**: A class of pharmaceuticals that kill bacteria. Over-presence in rivers causes antimicrobial resistance (AMR).
- **Cosmetics & Personal Care**: Compounds washed off during showering, such as UV filters, fragrances, and preservatives.

## Chemical Classes
- **Fatty Acids & Alcohols**: Often naturally occurring, but can be from industrial surfactants.
- **Carboxylic Acid**: Organic acids, extremely common in both natural metabolic pathways and industrial precursors.
- **Surfactant**: Compounds that lower surface tension (like soaps and detergents). High levels indicate untreated domestic wastewater.
